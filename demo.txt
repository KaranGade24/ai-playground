Notice that text can be split across chunks. If your application needs complete words or sentences, you may want to buffer partial text before tokenizing.

Your tokenizer

Instead of printing,

ontext(text) {
    console.log(text);
}

you would do

ontext(text) {
    tokenBuffer.push(...encode(text));
}

Now your token buffer grows.

tokenBuffer

[123,456,789,...]

Whenever it reaches 1000 tokens,

yield decode(tokenBuffer.splice(0, 1000));

The remaining tokens stay in memory.

Before

1200 tokens

After yield

200 tokens remain

Then parsing continues.
